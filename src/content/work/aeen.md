## Context

Eyewear is a visual product. The frame has to feel right before a shopper can imagine wearing it. AEEN’s work connects a recognisable identity with a clearer way to explore the catalogue.

## The challenge

A storefront needs more than product listings. Shape, styling, collection organisation and product confidence need to work together without making the mobile journey harder.

## The work

- Built an identity around AEEN’s burgundy palette and its Aeenie mascot.
- Worked on Shopify product-page customisation, Liquid, CSS and product information.
- Explored collection imagery for clearer frame-style navigation.
- Developed a virtual try-on experience for the Octa Metal frame.

## What changed

The brand work extends into an interactive product experience. The virtual try-on implementation is the most concrete technical example: a heavy source asset was prepared for browser use, with separate lens materials and face tracking.
