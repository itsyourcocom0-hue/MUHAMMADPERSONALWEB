## Context

A gadget store has a very different catalogue and purchase journey from fashion or eyewear — more specs, more compatibility questions, more "will this work with my phone?" messages. The same need remains: connect the store, the advertising and the operations.

## The scope

- Catalogue and product presentation.
- Storefront work and the buying journey.
- Advertising connected to product and fulfilment decisions.
- Day-to-day store operations.

## The AI support assistant

Repeated pre-sale questions eat into a small team's day. The answer was a self-contained AI customer-service chatbot with two sides:

- **An admin panel** where the team enters products, policies and FAQs.
- **A customer chat** that answers from that data — not from guesses.

This overview defines the scope of the work without presenting unverified revenue, return rates or advertising improvements as outcomes.
