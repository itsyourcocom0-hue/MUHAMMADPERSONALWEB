## 1. Make the product understandable

Open the page on a phone. Before scrolling, can you tell what the product is, what it costs and which variation is selected? Check whether the first image and title answer the same question.

## 2. Answer the objections near the decision

For eyewear, dimensions, fit, lens details and frame imagery matter. For a gadget, compatibility and what is included may matter more. Move useful answers close to the buying controls instead of burying them in general brand copy.

## 3. Inspect the complete journey

Select each variant, change quantity, add to cart and follow the checkout path. Note unexpected costs, unavailable variants, unclear delivery information and difficult mobile interactions.

## 4. Prioritise before changing things

Separate broken behaviour from missing information and visual preference. Fix a broken purchase action before debating a decorative element. Keep a record of the change and how you plan to assess it.
